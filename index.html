Where I stand

Four priorities built from two years of town halls, block walks, and budget hearings across Ward 12.

Affordable housing

Speed up approvals for below-market units and close the loopholes that let vacant lots sit empty for years.

Public safety & trust

Fund co-response teams for mental health calls and put crash data behind every new stop sign and crosswalk request.

Small business & main streets

Cut the permit backlog from 90 days to two weeks and support small businesses along New Sudbury and Lasalle.

Reliable roads & snow clearing

Get winter road maintenance back on schedule and fix the potholes that reappear every spring thaw.

About the candidate

A neighbor first, a councilmember second.

Linnet grew up in Ward 12 and has lived here for the past fifteen years, raising her family a few streets from where she went to school. She's spent the last decade working in Sudbury's mining supply sector and volunteering with the local ratepayers' association, where she learned how slow and confusing City Hall can be for the people it's supposed to serve.

She's running because Ward 12 deserves a councillor's office that answers the phone, shows up to the community meeting, and reads the budget line by line before voting on it.

— Linnet
